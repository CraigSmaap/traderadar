2. Root Cause Analysis

  • A
    No cooldown after SL hits. After a stop loss is triggered, the engine was immediately eligible to re-enter the same asset in the same direction on the next refresh cycle (every 30 minutes). During a sustained downtrend, this created a losing loop — BUY → SL → BUY → SL.
  • B
    Breakeven trades leave money on the table. 5 trades reached TP1, moved the stop to breakeven, then reversed to entry. The position closed at 0% instead of locking in the TP1 gain. These should be partial wins, not flat trades.
  • C
    Day 1 performance was an outlier. The 4% growth on launch day was caused by multiple signals hitting TP2+ simultaneously during a high-volatility session. This is not a repeatable baseline — typical daily expectancy with the current signal volume is 0.3–0.8% on winning days.

3. Fix Implemented — SL Cooldown

Deployed 22 May 2026 · market API route After any SL hit, the same asset + same direction is blocked from generating a new signal for 4 hours. The opposite direction on the same asset is still allowed immediately (e.g., if DE40 BUY hits SL, a DE40 SELL signal can still fire). Crypto is covered 24/7 including weekends since it is the only asset class that trades on Saturday and Sunday.

5. Active Signal Rules (as of 22 May 2026)

  • 1
    SL Cooldown (NEW): Same asset + same direction is blocked for 4 hours after any SL hit. Opposite direction is unaffected.
  • 2
    EMA(50) Trend Filter: BUY signals only fire when price is above EMA(50). SELL signals only fire when price is below EMA(50). This prevented entries against the medium-term trend.
  • 3
    RSI Guardrails: BUY blocked if RSI < 50 or RSI > 72 (overbought). SELL blocked if RSI > 50 or RSI < 28 (oversold). Avoids chasing exhausted moves.
  • 4
    Session Filter: Non-crypto signals only fire during London (09:00–18:00 SAST), New York (18:00–23:00 SAST), or overlap (14:00–18:00 SAST) sessions. No signals during off-hours or weekends for non-crypto.
  • 5
    US Equity Gate: NAS100 and US500 signals only fire during the US cash session (15:30–23:00 SAST) when liquidity is meaningful.
  • 6
    Correlation Cap: Maximum 2 signals per correlated group (e.g., NAS100 + US500 count as one group). Prevents over-exposure to a single macro move.
  • 7
    Max 5 new signals per refresh: The engine caps new signal generation at 5 per cycle to avoid flooding the portfolio with simultaneous entries.
  • 8
    Crypto runs 24/7: BTCUSD, ETHUSD, XRPUSD, and SOLUSD are exempt from session and weekend filters. SL cooldown still applies.
